One stray line — Disallow: / — can hide your entire site from Google. This free checker fetches your robots.txt, confirms it's reachable and parseable, flags a site-wide block, and tells you whether it points at your sitemap.

The costly mistakes it catches


Disallow: / (blocks everything — often left over from staging), a missing file (crawlers assume all-allowed, which may not be what you want), and no Sitemap: line (search engines crawl less efficiently).
